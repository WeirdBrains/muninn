Reviews

A review asks peers to look at one thing of yours: an entry, a journal and its entries, a project, a live search or a model record. Reviewers can view it, comment, suggest changes, highlight text and vote. You accept or reject the suggestions and merge the accepted ones, which makes a new numbered revision of the thing.

Asking for a review

mnnn review request journal "field notes" --note "before Friday" --allow comment,suggest
mnnn review request record trades/3f2a --label ben --peer /ip4/…/p2p/…
mnnn review invite "field notes" --label cleo

request takes a kind (entry, journal, project, search or live-search, record; plurals work too) and a name or id; a record is MODEL/RECORD. It makes the first link unless --no-link; invite makes more. With --peer the invitation is delivered to that node (the reviewer sees it in mnnn net requests), otherwise an invite link (muninn://join/…) is printed to pass on. The keys are exchanged on their own either way.

  • A link is a key exchange scoped to the review ({"kind":"resource","as":"review"}): good for one reviewer, for 7 days. A used link is refused to anyone else.
  • Permissions. --allow narrows what reviewers may do: comment, suggest, highlight, vote. view is always allowed; the default is everything.
  • Reviewers reach the review through your node, so keep it running (mnnn net start, or S in the TUI's network window).
  • Reports are not reviewable.

Reviewing

mnnn review join muninn://join/…        # the link, the review's title or the request id; `-` reads stdin
mnnn review view "field notes"
mnnn review comment "field notes" "a fine start"
mnnn review suggest "field notes" --entry 3f2a --at "the cat" --to "the comet" --why "it is a comet"
mnnn review highlight "field notes" --entry 3f2a --at "hill" --color yellow --note "lovely"
mnnn review vote "field notes" approve        # approve, changes or reject
mnnn review vote "field notes" up --on 9c1e   # up or down on an item
mnnn review withdraw "field notes" 9c1e
mnnn review sync "field notes"
mnnn review leave "field notes"

Every command taking REVIEW works on your own reviews and on the ones you joined (those go through the author's node). Anchors: --field (default: an entry's body, a journal's name, a project's description, a search's query; records name a field), --at TEXT with --nth N for the Nth occurrence, and --entry for an entry of a journal review. A suggestion replaces the --at text, or the whole field, with --to (--json for a record's typed value). Replies use comment --reply ITEM. Each person has one vote on the review and one on each item; voting again changes it.

Your copy, offline

Joining copies what is reviewed to your node. view and show refresh the copy when the author's node answers and show the copy otherwise. Comments, suggestions, highlights and votes made while it is offline are kept and sent on the next mnnn review sync (a running node also does this every few minutes). Kept work the author's node refuses is dropped, with a warning. A record review also creates its model on your node, or adds the fields yours is missing. Fields are only ever added, never changed or removed, and each change is recorded in history as review.model. The copy itself can't be edited. New revisions refresh it.

When the review is closed, you are dropped or you leave, the copy stays, archived and read-only. mnnn review remove forgets it, together with any work still waiting.

Accepting and merging

mnnn review show "field notes"
mnnn review accept "field notes" 9c1e 4b2d     # or --all
mnnn review reject "field notes" --all
mnnn review merge "field notes"                # --force applies suggestions that no longer fit
mnnn review revisions journal "field notes"

Only suggestions are accepted or rejected. A merge applies the accepted ones and gives each changed thing (the entry, journal, project, search or record) a new revision with its before and after values and the items in it. A suggestion whose quoted text is gone, or whose whole field changed since it was made, is a conflict: it stays accepted and is reported, unless --force.

close ends the review: the reviewers' exchanges are revoked and they are told. reopen opens it again; reviewers need new links. drop REVIEW REVIEWER takes one reviewer off. remove deletes a review and its thread (it asks first); revisions stay. For a review you joined, remove forgets it.

The Reviews window (TUI)

V, or Projects ▸ Reviews (Reviews…, Ask for a review…, Thread…, Reviewing (joined)…). Pages: 1 My reviews, 2 Thread, 3 Reviewing; Tab / ← → switch, j / k move, g refresh, Esc closes.

page keys
My reviews n ask for a review (journal NAME, record MODEL/RECORD, …), Enter its thread, i a link (optionally delivered to a peer: its node address or name), p view, H revisions, o close / reopen, d delete (y confirms)
Thread c comment, e suggest, h highlight, v vote, r reply, a / x accept / reject the selected suggestion, A / X all, m merge, F merge anyway, w withdraw, D drop the last joined reviewer; i, p, H as above
Reviewing j join a link, Enter view and thread, s sync, c / e / h / v add items, l leave (y confirms)

Item prompts take optional field=F entry=E nth=N first, then @quoted text :: the rest to anchor it: entry=3f2a @the cat :: the comet suggests a replacement. Without @ the whole field is meant. A vote is approve, changes or reject, or up ITEM / down ITEM.

History and rules

Every step is in the history under the current author: review.request, review.invite, review.join, review.joined, review.comment, review.suggest, review.highlight, review.vote, review.withdraw, review.accept, review.reject, review.merge, review.close, review.closed, review.reopen, review.drop, review.leave, review.remove and review.serve. A merge also records the edit itself (entry.edit, journal.edit, project.edit, search.edit or record.update) with the review's id. Rules can react to all of them (mnnn rule events).

More commands

mnnn review list [--json]        # your reviews and the ones you joined
mnnn review close REVIEW         # close it; reviewers' links and exchanges end, their copies stay read-only
mnnn review reopen REVIEW        # open a closed review again; reviewers need new links
